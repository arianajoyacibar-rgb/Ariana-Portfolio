# SagipAlert

A disaster-preparedness app for a barangay or school: alerts, plain-language guidance for typhoons, floods and earthquakes, a go-bag checklist, a family plan, an evacuation map and community reports.

## Features
- Alert banner with the PAGASA tropical cyclone wind signal table (#1 to #5) for reference
- Before / during / after guidance for typhoon, flood and earthquake
- Go-bag checklist with progress, saved on the device
- Evacuation map: click where you live and see the nearest evacuation centers by distance
- Family plan with emergency contacts and a meeting place
- Community reports (flood, blocked road, need help, safe)
- Online/offline indicator; all data is local, so the app keeps working without a connection
- One-tap emergency number dialog (911)

## Run
Open `index.html`.

## Important
The alert, map, places and reports are **fictional practice data**. This is a prototype, not a source of official warnings. For real alerts follow PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC and your local DRRMO.
