Quick rules that always apply

  • Banks, GCash, Maya and couriers never ask for your OTP, PIN or password.
  • Open the official app yourself instead of tapping a link in a message.
  • A prize you did not enter for, or a fee to release a parcel, is a scam signal.
  • If it makes you rush, stop. Real accounts are not closed in 24 hours by a text.
