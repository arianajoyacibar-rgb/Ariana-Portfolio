    Talk to someone

    You do not have to wait for a crisis. If something is weighing on you, you can leave a message for the guidance office. You can stay anonymous.

    Prototype note: in this demo the message is saved only on this device. A real version would deliver it to a trained counselor with your school's consent process.

    Need to talk right now?

    • NCMH Crisis Hotline (24/7, free): 1553 from a landline, or 0917-899-8727
    • Emergency: 911
    • Or tell a trusted adult, teacher or your school's guidance counselor.

    Your privacy

    • ✓Your entries are saved only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
    • ✓The mood analysis runs on your device with a simple word list. No text is sent to a server.
    • ✓Prompts to reach out are suggestions, not labels. Kalma does not diagnose.
    • ✓You can export or delete everything at any time.
    • ✓A school version would need written consent, a data-protection review under the Data Privacy Act, and a clear rule for who can see what.
